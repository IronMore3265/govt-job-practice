# Sample: Mathematics with LaTeX (for testing the importer, not real content)

Math is written between dollar signs. A literal dollar sign is written \$.

### Q1. What is the value of $\frac{3}{4} + \frac{1}{4}$?

- A. $\frac{1}{2}$
- B. $1$
- C. $\frac{4}{8}$
- D. $2$

**Answer:** B

**Reason:** $\frac{3}{4} + \frac{1}{4} = \frac{4}{4} = 1$.

### Q2. What is $\sqrt{144}$?

- A. $11$
- B. $12$
- C. $14$
- D. $72$

**Answer:** B

**Reason:** $12 \times 12 = 144$, so $\sqrt{144} = 12$.

### Q3. A book costs \$40 after a 20% discount. What was the marked price in dollars?

- A. 48
- B. 50
- C. 52
- D. 60

**Answer:** B

**Reason:** The marked price is $\frac{40}{0.8} = 50$.

### Q4. Solve $x^2 - 5x + 6 = 0$. The roots are:

- A. $2$ and $3$
- B. $-2$ and $-3$
- C. $1$ and $6$
- D. $-1$ and $-6$

**Answer:** A

**Reason:**
$$x^2 - 5x + 6 = (x-2)(x-3)$$
So the roots are $x = 2$ and $x = 3$.
