# The Determiners
## i. The Determiners
যেসব word কোনো noun বা pronoun-এর পূর্বে বসে তাকে নির্দেশ করে তাদেরকে Determiner বলে।
**Classification of determiners:** Determiners তিন প্রকার। যথা:
### 1. Central Determiner
**Articles:** ইংরেজি ভাষায় সবচেয়ে বেশি ব্যবহৃত determiner হলো definite and indefinite article (a, an, the).
- Runa is __a__ student. This is __an__ orange.
- __The__ man is good.
**Demonstratives:** This, that, these, those.
- __This__ book is interesting. I like __these__ people.
- __That__ mango is ripe.
**Possessives:** My, our, your, his, her, their etc.
- __My__ pencil is fine. We love __our__ country.
- I want __your__ book.
**Quantifiers:** Some, any, no, enough, either, neither, much, many, each, every, etc.
- I have __enough__ rice.
- Do you have __any__ book?
- I want __neither__ book.
### 2. Predeterminer
যে determiner গুলো central determiner-এর পূর্বে বসে তাদেরকে predeterminer বলা হয়। both, all, half, double, one-third, such, what etc.
- I bought __both__ the books. He knows __all__ the boys.
- I want __double__ the amount.
- __What__ a fine day it is!
### 3. Postdeterminer
যে determiner গুলো central determiner-এর পরে বসে তাদেরকে postdeterminer বলে।
**Cardinal Numbers:** one(a), two, three, four, etc.
- I like the __three__ books. He bought the __two__ cars.
**Ordinal Numbers:** first, second, third, etc.
- Karim is the __first__ boy.
- March is the __third__ month of the year.
**Closed-class Quantifiers:** few, large, etc.
- I have a few books. Give me the little milk.
**Open-class or Phrasal Quantifiers:** lot of, deal of, amount of, great deal of, etc.
- I have a __lot of__ books.
- He has a __great deal of__ money.
