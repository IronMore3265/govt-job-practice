# Sample: English grammar (for testing the importer, not real content)

## Part A: Articles and tenses

### Q1. Choose the correct sentence.

- A. She don't like tea.
- B. She doesn't like tea.
- C. She not like tea.
- D. She doesn't likes tea.

**Answer:** B

**Reason:** With a third person singular subject, the negative uses "doesn't" and the verb stays in its base form.

### Q2. Fill in the blank: He is ___ honest man.

- A. a
- B. an
- C. the
- D. no article

**Answer:** B

**Reason:** "Honest" begins with a vowel sound because the h is silent, so "an" is used. See page 14 for the rule on silent letters.

**Page:** 14

---

### Q3. The synonym of "rapid" is:

- A. slow
- B. quick
- C. heavy
- D. silent

**Answer:** B

**Reason:** "Rapid" and "quick" both mean fast.
A second line of the reason is allowed and joins the first.

### Q4. Which word is a noun?

- A. quickly
- B. happiness
- C. beautiful
- D. run

**Answer:** B

**Reason:** "Happiness" names a feeling, so it is a noun.
